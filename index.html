How to play
  1. One player creates a room and shares the four-letter code.
  2. One player is the Medium. They see the séance table and its cursed relics, but not the book.
  3. Everyone else is a Scholar. They read the grimoire, but never see the table.
  4. Talk it through — in the same room or on a voice call — and cleanse every relic before the spirit's wrath boils over.
